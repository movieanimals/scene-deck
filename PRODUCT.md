# Scene Deck
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
Native macOS (SwiftUI).
## Stack
SwiftUI and Foundation, preferred explicitly in the brief. No third-party dependencies.
## Users and purpose
Derek wants quick access to existing Philips Hue Bridge scenes beyond Apple Home's limited surface. Scenes are the primary workflow.
## Capabilities and constraints
Full window, menu bar companion, room/zone grouping, favorites, scene recall, group brightness, and a foundation for live Bridge state. Demo works offline. No real Bridge discovery, pairing, credentials, or light writes without later explicit approval. Bridge model unknown. No external assets, installs, accounts, publishing, or signing account changes.
## Evidence
The supplied brief is the product authority. Demo rooms and scenes are illustrative, not Derek's real home. Hue v2 reference and TLS guidance returned 403 during research; unverified integration details must remain documented and gated.
## Assumptions
App name Scene Deck and a dark evening-use palette are implementation choices, not claimed user preferences. Existing scenes precede scene creation, editing, gallery imports, or dynamic controls.
